# MANDATE — a Downing Street simulation

Lead a party. Take the country. Survive the maths.

Career mode: start as Prime Minister — or as Leader of the Opposition hunting
an AI government, with the mountain sized by your party. Six playable parties
including the SNP (independence win-condition). Seven scenarios (Sterling
Crisis, Knife-Edge, The Cold Wind, minority government…), three difficulties.

Six rooms: Overview (events, front pages, poll tracker) · Treasury (17 fiscal
levers with Laffer curves feeding NHS waits, crime, migration, housing) ·
Parliament (bills through animated Commons divisions, snap elections) ·
World (18-region situation board: summits, sanctions, covert ops, deployments,
wars) · Party (factions, reshuffles, pledges) · Media (five outlets to court).

Play: https://felixgbondatpropnition.github.io/mandate/

- `index.html` + `style.css` + `data.js` + `engine.js` + `ui.js` — no build, no deps
- `test.js` — headless fleet: 140 full careers across the whole matrix (`node test.js`)

Daily Crisis mode seeds the date — identical timeline for everyone, compare legacies.
